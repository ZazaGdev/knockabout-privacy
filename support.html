Knockabout Support

Knockabout is a party game for three or more people in the same room. No account, no sign-up, nothing to subscribe to.

Contact

Email zazagdev@gmail.com. We aim to reply within three working days.

When something has gone wrong in a game, the most useful things to tell us are which game you were playing, whether you were all on one phone or on separate phones, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, no password and no email address to give. Open the app and play.

Do I need an internet connection? Not for the games played on one shared phone. Those work in airplane mode. Games where everyone uses their own phone need a connection, because the phones have to reach each other.

How do people join my room? You are shown a four-character code. Everyone else opens the app, taps Join, and types it. You all need to be able to hear each other, because the game is played out loud in the same room.

Is it free? Yes, all of it. There is nothing to buy, no subscription and no advertising.

What happens to the photos I pick? Only the one photo on screen leaves your phone, and only in the photo game. It is deleted when the round ends. Location and other embedded information is stripped before it is sent, and the upload is refused outright if any is still present. The app never reads your photo library; your phone's own picker hands over the single image you chose.

How do I delete my data? There is no account to delete. Leaving a room deletes everything from it within the hour. To clear what is stored on your own phone, use Clear history in the Profile screen, or uninstall the app.

A player's phone has gone dark and the game is stuck. While the room is waiting on somebody, everyone else sees their name with a Skip button beside it, and the room carries on. Skipping is not removing: they keep their seat, and they can come back and keep playing. The photo game works differently, because nobody there is told whose photo is on screen: a countdown closes the round on its own instead. If a room still will not move, everyone can leave and start a fresh one with a new code, and nothing is lost but the round in progress.

Something is wrong with the content of a prompt. Tell us at zazagdev@gmail.com and quote the prompt. We would rather cut a line than have it read out in somebody's living room.
